House Guidelines

A few simple rules to keep Didoo's Evergreen Café a warm and wonderful place for everyone.

No drugs on premises

Didoo's is a strictly drug-free space. Use or possession of any substance is not permitted on or around our premises.

Right to refuse service

We reserve the right to refuse service to ensure a safe and comfortable environment for all guests.

Age verification

Some menu items are available to guests aged 21 and above only. Valid ID may be requested.
